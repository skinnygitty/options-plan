Reading the flow

Free data can't prove a trade was bought or sold. The lean comes from where the last trade printed between bid and ask; open interest the next morning tells you whether it was new money.

What printedUsually means
Calls bought, OI up next dayBullish, new money
Puts sold, OI upBullish; the seller is happy to own lower
Puts bought, OI upBearish, or a hedge on stock if price is at highs
Calls sold, OI upA cap overhead; often covered calls
Big volume, OI downPositions closing; not a new signal

On the line: the diamond is the entry, red is the stop and risk zone, green is the reward zone out to the targets, the ring is the last close, and grey ticks are the call and put walls.
